## Brand & Style

This design system drives a universal sports discovery and elite coaching intelligence platform. The visual voice balances high-performance athletic rigor with high-end sports editorial poise. It avoids the dark, hyper-gamified tropes of generic betting or fitness trackers, opting instead for the clarity, precision, and authority of premium sports media publications and scouting software.

### Tone & Demeanor
- **Analytical & Direct:** Instantaneous readability of athlete metrics, video feedback, and training loads.
- **Dynamic & Athletic:** Energetic accents evoke speed and precision without introducing sensory overload.
- **Executive Editorial:** Clean grids, crisp lines, structured typographic contrast, and restrained elevation establish trust for scouts, directors, and elite coaches.

### Design Movement: Modern Precision Editorial
A synthesis of Swiss modernist layout structure, contemporary sports broadcast graphics, and clean tactile SaaS. Surfaces rely on pure, luminous neutrals, razor-thin framing borders, restrained ambient elevation, and selective high-chroma kinetic accents to direct focus.

## Colors

The palette establishes an unmistakable hierarchy built on high-contrast legibility, tactical status signaling, and luminous surface separation.

### Core Canvas & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50). An ultra-clean, desaturated cool gray providing soft optical contrast against elevated cards.
- **Card & Sheet Surface:** `#FFFFFF`. True white for cards, panels, modals, and elevated tiles to provide immediate pop and structure.
- **Subtle Surface Container:** `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100). Reserved for table headers, segmented control tracks, and input disabled fills.
- **Hairline Structural Borders:** `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200). 1px crisp borders delineating cards, dividers, and data grids without visual clutter.

### Primary Accents & Hierarchy
- **Electric Cobalt (`#1D4ED8`):** The primary brand and action color. Drives active states, primary CTA buttons, timeline needles, selected navigation tabs, and primary metrics.
- **Cobalt Interactive Hover:** `#1E40AF` (Slate/Blue shade for interactive tap/click feedback).
- **Cobalt Subdued:** `#EFF6FF` (Blue 50). Background fill for active navigational items, tags, and selected roster rows.

### Functional Performance Colors
- **Emerald/Teal (`#0D9488` / `#10B981`):** Represents positive performance metrics, velocity gains, readiness ratings, and completed drills. Paired with `#F0FDF4` for badge backgrounds.
- **Refined Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Drives training warnings, contract expirations, pending athlete reviews, and strain alerts. Paired with `#FFFBEB` for tint backgrounds.
- **Critical Crimson (`#EF4444`):** Indicates injury flags, acute overtraining thresholds, and negative delta variances.

### Typography & Neutrals
- **Display & Headings:** `#0F172A` (Slate 900) for authoritative contrast and zero eye fatigue.
- **Body & Secondary Copy:** `#334155` (Slate 700) and `#64748B` (Slate 500) for metadata, scouting notes, and secondary indicators.

## Typography

The typographic strategy pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for display, headlines, athlete telemetry metrics, and badges with **Inter** for data tables, observational notes, and multi-line body text.

### Implementation Rules
- **Numerical Telemetry:** All standalone statistical figures (e.g., sprint splits, VO2 max, rep counts) use `metric-display` in `Plus Jakarta Sans` with tabular figures enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal jitter during real-time tracking.
- **Case Hierarchy:** Category chips, scouting grade tags, and table header labels use `label-xs` in uppercase with subtle letter-spacing (`0.05em`) to evoke sports telemetry equipment.
- **Editorial Balance:** Maintain tight tracking on large headings to evoke contemporary sports publishing, shifting to standard tracking on `Inter` body text for clarity in low-light field conditions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column fluid grid system geared towards dense athletic dashboards, split-screen video review panels, and side-by-side scouting comparisons.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12-column layout with 24px gutters and 32px canvas margins. Side navigation persists as a 260px fixed rail.
- **Tablet / Small Desktop (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with 16px gutters and 24px margins. Navigation collapses into an icon-only slim rail (72px) or top app bar.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with 12px gutters and 16px lateral canvas margins. Persistent bottom app bar with thumb-zone optimization.

### Architectural Density
- **Micro-Gaps:** Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for tightly grouped stats (e.g., athlete avatar next to player number and position badge).
- **Component Padding:** Standard cards employ `space-lg` (24px) on desktop, tapering to `space-md` (16px) on mobile viewports to maximize usable chart real estate.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain an editorial and pristine field-ready aesthetic, elevation prioritizes pure surface boundary contrasts, hairline perimeter borders, and exceptionally subtle diffuse shadows rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation, non-interactive base plane.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Default):** `#FFFFFF` fill framed by a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border. Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Hovered Tiles):** `#FFFFFF` fill with border transition to `#CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Flyouts, Popovers & Dropdowns):** `#FFFFFF` surface with 1px `#CBD5E1` border. Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 4 (Modal Overlays & Quick-Action Drawers):** `#FFFFFF` elevated above a backdrop scrim tinted with `#0F172A` at 40% opacity with an active `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

### Optical Grounding
No component relies solely on shadow for demarcation; the hairline `#E2E8F0` edge must always be preserved to guarantee surface integrity across calibrated and non-calibrated tactical displays.

## Shapes

The design system uses a balanced `roundedness: 2` scale (8px standard corner radius), marrying architectural sharpness with modern handheld software ergonomics.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Standard UI Elements (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Primary buttons, input controls, metric cards, video thumbnail frames, and scouting report cards.
- **Container / Group Containers (`1rem` / 16px):** Outer layout cards, complex multi-metric modules, bottom sheets, and modal frames.
- **Pill Formats (`9999px`):** Athlete state badges, position indicators (e.g., `CB`, `PG`), metric status chips, and filter toggles.
- **Compact Badges (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Tactical video timestamp tags and micro sport notation flags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1D4ED8`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `Plus Jakarta Sans` 600. Flat fill with 1px border `#1E40AF`. Hover shifts to `#1E40AF`.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`, 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border. Hover shifts background to `#F8FAFC` and border to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Transparent background, text `#334155`, hover background `#F1F5F9`.
- **Sizing:** Large (44px height for mobile touch targets), Medium (38px height for desktop toolbars), Small (30px height for inline table actions).

### Sports Badges & Status Chips
- **Position Badges:** High-contrast, compact pills (e.g., `FWD`, `GK`). Height 22px, padding 0 8px, background `#F1F5F9`, text `#0F172A`, font `label-xs` uppercase bold.
- **Performance Badges:** 
  - *Positive/Peak:* Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, hairline border `#A7F3D0`.
  - *Warning/Load Alert:* Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, hairline border `#FDE68A`.
  - *Fatigue/Injury:* Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, hairline border `#FECACA`.

### Compact Metric Cards
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, corner radius `0.5rem`.
- Padding `1rem`. Top metadata row displays the label in `label-sm` (`#64748B`) alongside a contextual Lucide icon (16px, `#94A3B8`).
- Center value rendered in `metric-display` (`#0F172A`).
- Sub-row features an inline trend indicator chip with directional arrow (e.g., `+4.2% vs baseline` in `#0D9488`).

### Filter Tabs & Segmented Controls
- Contained pills or segmented bars housed inside a `#F1F5F9` track with 4px padding.
- Selected item pops with a pure white elevated pill (`#FFFFFF`), crisp hairline border (`#E2E8F0`), subtle shadow, and `#0F172A` text.
- Inactive items retain transparent backgrounds with `#64748B` text.

### Form Inputs
- Background `#FFFFFF`, height 40px, corner radius `0.5rem`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`.
- Text color `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state: Outline-free, border color `#1D4ED8`, subtle ring glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.12)`.

### Roster Lists & Tables
- Border-separated rows with 1px `#F1F5F9` dividers.
- Hover state across entire row triggers `#F8FAFC`.
- Cells maintain strict vertical alignment with tabular figures for instant vertical scanning.

### Mobile Navigation & Drawers
- **Bottom Navigation Bar:** Height 64px, background `#FFFFFF` with top border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`. 4 to 5 key destinations with Lucide icons (22px) and `label-xs` text.
- **Action Drawer:** Slides up from canvas base, 16px radius on top corners, drag-handle bar (36px width, 4px height, `#CBD5E1`) centered at top margin.
